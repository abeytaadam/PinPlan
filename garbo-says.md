# Garbo says

The one thing to remember for each game, in your own words. Write a single line under a game's
heading and it replaces the "Garbo says" line on that game's page and its card, at every venue.
Leave a game empty to keep the researched line.

- One line per game. **Double asterisks** make text bold, *single asterisks* italic.
- Keep the "## game-id" lines exactly as they are. The name in brackets is only a reminder.
- Edit this file right on GitHub (open it, press the pencil, then "Commit changes").
  The site picks it up within a minute or two.

## attack-from-mars  (Attack From Mars)
Use your super skill shot to hit the targets in front of the saucer to drop the gate. Start smashing those saucers! Not safe? Go multiball or TOTAL ANNIHILATION.

## avengers-infinity-quest  (Avengers: Infinity Quest)
1. Bring a portal lock into reality gem quest. 2. Bash Thor for multiball. 3. Profit.

## back-to-the-future  (Back to the Future)
Repeated ramp shots start unlimited millions mode. Loop the left ramp unless you can't find it. Then try for DMC multiball.

## cactus-canyon  (Cactus Canyon)
Gold Mine Multiball supers are juicy. If you're feeling froggy, try for Showdown Multiball. (4 Quickdraw Targets)

## centaur  (Centaur)
Collect orbs! Release orbs! Stay in multiball! Destroy centaur!

## deadpool  (Deadpool)
If you reach Mech Suit Multiball, you're having a good game. Combo shots for weapons and collect at the scoop!

## dune  (Dune)

## dungeons-and-dragons  (Dungeons & Dragons: The Tyrant's Eye)
SHIELD IS YOUR FRIEND! Check to see if the game is dialed in enough to hit shots while the ball is resting on the shield while it's raised.

## elvira-party-monsters  (Elvira and the Party Monsters)

## evil-dead  (Evil Dead)

Boomstick multiball can be big with a mode stack. Holding up the right sling flipper as the ball comes down the wireform makes for easy right flipper traps.

## fallout  (Fallout)

Picking Maximus grants more time in your Power Armor (2x Playfield). Backhand Deathclaw for multiball. Try to start a mode before hitting that last shot.

## fish-tales  (Fish Tales)
Monster Fish or Multiball? That is the question. Shoot the boat for the former, the fishing reel lock for the latter.

## foo-fighters  (Foo Fighters)
Garbo likes to start with New Orleans, then L.A., then Portland. Finish 3 van modes to qualify mini wizard mode.

## game-of-thrones  (Game of Thrones)
Soft plunge, catch right flipper. Post pass, shoot green lock targets. Up the middle for a lock. Repeat. If you mess it up when you plunge, check if your playfield is validated, if not, drain and try again!

## ghostbusters  (Ghostbusters)
Big scores happen in 6x playfield. Modes on the left scoop only require 2 completions to light miniwizard mode. That scoop do be tricky though sometimes.

## godzilla  (Godzilla)
Skillshot for ballsave then hit ramps twice to light battle. Go for Gigan if the right ramp is doable. Ski-pass and repeat it to take him down quickly!

## guardians-of-the-galaxy  (Guardians of the Galaxy)
Most people like to start with Quill for the quick multiball. Garbo prefers Yaka. Smash Groot for locks and get multiball prepped for the next mode! BOBALEEBO.

## indiana-jones-the-pinball-adventure  (Indiana Jones: The Pinball Adventure)

## iron-maiden  (Iron Maiden: Legacy of the Beast)

## john-wick  (John Wick)

## king-kong  (King Kong: Myth of Terror Island)

Like a lot of classic games, knock down inlane drop targets for 5x bonus and then rip the lit spinner. Super bonus carries over so try to lock it in ball one.

Take ball locks or start modes with your skillshot. Jackpots and super jackpots are big points.

## revenge-from-mars  (Revenge From Mars)
